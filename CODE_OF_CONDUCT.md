# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RxRelay a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for patients, operators, and the community
- Taking responsibility and apologizing for mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Using the project to enable unconsented outreach or unsafe medical automation

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub (`@vnmoorthy`). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviors they deem inappropriate.
