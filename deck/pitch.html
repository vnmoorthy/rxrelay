Patient speaks naturally. RxRelay captures narrow, explicit coordination consent.

02 · Coordinate

RxRelay / 08

Trust by construction

09 / 12

What we refuse.

Aggressively useful inside a narrow boundary — visibly humble outside it.

Can do

  • Record explicit coordination consent
  • Check non-clinical status
  • Document counterpart outcomes
  • Send consented patient updates

Will not do

  • Clinical advice or dosing guidance
  • Change or transfer prescriptions
  • Disclose controlled-medication inventory
  • Contact unconsented recipients

The wow factor is knowing when not to act.
